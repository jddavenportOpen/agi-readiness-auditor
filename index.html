How it works (and what it does NOT claim)

The tool encodes real, citable frameworks as rubrics — each criterion has a specific bar and a source URL. It gathers a company's public claims, maps claims→criteria with a skeptical, evidence-bound approach (LLM or deterministic keyword fallback — always produces a report either way), and scores whether the public posture meets each bar. It does not measure internal practice or whether commitments are honored.
